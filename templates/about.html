Rural Healthcare AI Initiative

Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India

Empowering Primary Health Centres (PHCs) with transparent, accessible, and clinically calibrated computer vision to prevent diabetes-related blindness.

Launch Screening
The Rural Healthcare Crisis

India is widely recognized as the diabetes capital of the world, with over 101 million citizens living with diabetes and an additional 136 million with pre-diabetes. Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) is a microvascular complication of diabetes that damages the delicate blood vessels of the retina.

Over 70% of India's population resides in rural areas, while over 80% of all trained ophthalmologists and retinal specialists practice in tier-1 and tier-2 urban centers. Most rural Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and Community Health Centres (CHCs) have no dilated retinal examination facilities. Consequently, rural patients are often diagnosed only at late, sight-threatening stages (Grade 3 or 4) when irreversible visual loss has already set in.

Clinical Reality: Over 90% of diabetic retinopathy blindness is preventable through timely, periodic dilated screening and early referral.
Project Objectives & Solution
  • Accessible Point-of-Care Triage: Enable Community Health Officers (CHOs) and rural health workers to capture fundus photography with portable cameras and receive real-time risk grading.
  • Automated Quality Safeguards: Prevent garbage-in, garbage-out errors by automatically screening for blur (Laplacian variance), under/overexposure, and retinal field coverage before classification.
  • Explainable AI via Grad-CAM: Provide visual heatmap justifications for every prediction, eliminating "black box" distrust and showing exactly where the neural network detected microvascular anomalies.
  • Standardized Referral Protocols: Provide structured clinical guidance based on International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) criteria to direct urgent cases to district hospitals.
  • Multilingual & Low-Bandwidth Architecture: Support English, Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), and Hindi (हिन्दी) with offline-capable CPU execution suitable for rural clinics.

International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) Grading
Grade Clinical Stage Retinal Fundus Findings PHC Referral Protocol
Grade 0 No DR No microaneurysms, hemorrhages or abnormalities Annual Screening
Grade 1 Mild NPDR Microaneurysms only (isolated focal red dots) Follow-up 6-12 Mo
Grade 2 Moderate NPDR Multiple microaneurysms, blot hemorrhages, hard exudates Referral ≤ 3 Months
Grade 3 Severe NPDR 4-2-1 Rule: >20 hemorrhages in 4 quadrants, venous beading Urgent: 2-4 Weeks
Grade 4 Proliferative DR Neovascularization (NVD/NVE), vitreous / preretinal hemorrhage Emergency: 48-72 Hr
Technical Integrity & Limitations
1. Dataset Provenance: Designed for weights fine-tuned on public benchmarks including APTOS 2019 (Aravind Eye Hospital cohort) and EyePACS.
2. Transparent Operational Mode: Unlike systems that disguise random weights, DR Vision AI explicitly reports when running in calibrated demonstration mode vs. certified production model weights.
3. Offline-First PHC Resiliency: Operates entirely on standard CPU machines without needing high-bandwidth cloud connectivity, ensuring rural clinics without reliable internet remain fully operational.
4. Future Scope: Integration with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) health IDs, automated diabetic macular edema (DME) optical coherence tomography triage, and tele-consultation video links with district hospital ophthalmologists.
